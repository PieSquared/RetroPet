For purchase links and component details, refer to the [Bill of Materials (BOM.csv)](main/BOM.csv).

# Assembly

**Notes:** Adhesive (such as superglue) may be required to secure the magnet inserts in place. Make sure the magnets are correctly aligned before attaching the enclosure parts.

### 1. Raspberry Pi Enclosure
1. Insert the Raspberry Pi into the main enclosure (the part with the V logo and Raspberry Pi logo).
2. Insert 4 magnets into the designated magnet inserts.

### 2. Top Enclosure
3. Insert 4 magnets into the top enclosure piece (the hat-shaped part).
4. Attach the top enclosure to the Raspberry Pi enclosure.

### 3. Monitor Enclosure
5. Insert 4 magnets into the top part of the monitor-shaped enclosure.
6. Insert 4 magnets into the bottom of the monitor-shaped enclosure.
7. Snap the monitor enclosure pieces together.

### 4. Display Assembly
8. Insert 4 magnets into the outer magnet inserts.
9. Place the touchscreen LCD into the display opening.
10. Secure the display using tape.

### 5. Final Assembly
11. Attach the completed monitor assembly to the display assembly.
12. Make sure all enclosure pieces are securely connected.
